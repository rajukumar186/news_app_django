The People's Era

User Manual for website visitors, contributors, advertisers, and administrators

1. Overview

The People's Era is a modern news and community platform where users can read the latest updates, discover local stories, explore promotional opportunities, submit support requests, and interact with the admin team.

The platform includes:

  • News and article browsing
  • Login, signup, and password recovery
  • Contact form with email OTP verification
  • Promotion and advertisement management
  • ID card creation and approval flow
  • Admin dashboard and support chat

2. Getting Started

Open the website

Visit the homepage and explore the latest stories, categories, and featured content.

Create an account

  • Click Login or Sign Up from the website header.
  • Enter your name, email, and password.
  • Complete the registration process and log in to access personalized actions.

Login and logout

  • Use your registered email and password to log in.
  • After login, access your profile and interactive services.
  • Use the logout option from the user menu to sign out securely.

3. Reading News and Content

  • Browse the homepage for trending stories and featured posts.
  • Use category buttons or filters to view relevant news sections.
  • Click a story title to open the full article page.
  • Use the share or quick action buttons to interact with the content.
Tip: Keep your browser updated for the best experience with article pages, media, and forms.

4. Contact Form and OTP Verification

  1. Open the Contact page.
  2. Select the subject that matches your purpose, such as support, advertisement, legal query, or general inquiry.
  3. Enter your name, email, and message.
  4. Request an OTP code. A one-time password is sent to the email provided.
  5. Verify the OTP before submitting the contact request.

If you do not receive the code, check your spam folder and click the resend option.

5. Forgot Password and Account Recovery

  • Click Forgot Password from the login popup or login page.
  • Enter the email linked to your account.
  • Follow the password reset link sent to your inbox.
  • Set a new password and return to the login screen.

6. Promotions and Advertising

Business users can submit promotion requests through the platform for advertising and sponsored visibility.

  • Go to the advertisement or promo section.
  • Fill in your business details and choose a plan.
  • Submit the request for admin review.
  • Admins review and activate or reject requests depending on approval status.

Approved promotions appear as active campaign slots and are shown to visitors.

7. ID Card and Employee Flow

Employees may create or update their ID card information for verification.

  • Open the ID card generation section.
  • Fill in your employee details carefully.
  • Submit the form to initiate verification.
  • Admins review and approve or reject the request.
  • Approved cards can be viewed or verified using the generated card link.

8. Admin Dashboard

Super admins can manage the platform from the control panel.

  • Review statistics and recent records.
  • Manage users, promotions, notices, videos, and news updates.
  • Open support chat with users and respond to live requests.
  • Review pending promotion requests and employee ID approvals.
  • Open model management pages for records and site management tasks.

9. Support Chat and Messaging

Users can start support conversations and admins can respond using the built-in chat desk.

  • Choose a room or user thread from the support desk.
  • Open the chat popup and send a message.
  • Admins can see timestamps and status details for sent messages.

10. Best Practices

  • Use a valid email address for all account and contact submissions.
  • Keep messages clear and specific when contacting support.
  • Check pending promotions and approvals regularly if you are managing the platform.
  • Do not share OTP codes with anyone else.

11. FAQ and Help

For common concerns and quick answers, visit the FAQ page. If you cannot find the answer you need, use the contact form and provide a detailed description of the issue.

Support channels: contact page, admin help desk, and email verification workflow.
